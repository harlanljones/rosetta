What it cannot do
  • A stat-season cell can lose even when the pooled result improves.
  • AAA → MLB is validated here; KBO and NPB are not held-out validated.
  • It selects players who got MLB time; it is not a promotion or injury model.
  • Intervals resample rows, not player clusters, so they are approximate.
  • Target-season outcomes are partial-season when the season is in progress.
How it works

Ratio-of-means link factors from real transferred-player pairs, an era floor, bootstrap resamples, and {{ meta.calibration }} calibration fit only on earlier target seasons. This test trains only on pairs with to_season < {{ meta.backtest_target_season }}. Synthetic rows never enter the public board.
