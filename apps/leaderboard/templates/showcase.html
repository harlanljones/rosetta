What it cannot do
  • FIP loses in 2024; pooled FIP is still close.
  • AAA → MLB is validated here; KBO and NPB are not held-out validated.
  • It selects players who got MLB time; it is not a promotion or injury model.
  • Intervals resample rows, not player clusters, so they are approximate.
  • 2026 outcomes are partial-season.
How it works

Ratio-of-means link factors from real transferred-player pairs, a 2019 AAA era floor, and 250 bootstrap resamples. This test trains only on pairs with to_season < {{ meta.backtest_target_season }}. Synthetic rows never enter the public board.
