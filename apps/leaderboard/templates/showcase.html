What it can’t do

  • FIP loses in 2024; pooled FIP is still close (1.027 vs 1.016 across 2022–26).
  • This validates AAA → MLB only. KBO and NPB translations are not held-out validated here.
  • It selects players who got MLB time; it is not a promotion, playing-time, or injury model.
  • Intervals resample rows, not player clusters, so they are approximate.
  • 2026 outcomes are partial-season.

How it works

Rosetta estimates ratio-of-means link factors from real transferred-player pairs, applies a 2019 AAA era floor to avoid mixing run environments, and bootstraps 250 times for uncertainty. For this test, it trains only on pairs with to_season < {{ meta.backtest_target_season }}. Synthetic rows never enter the public board.
