How to read one player

field guide

Signed error = prediction − actual. Positive means the model overshot the outcome; negative means it undershot. Improvement = absolute AAA-prior miss − absolute model miss, so positive is a win over simply carrying the prior forward. Error buckets use stat-specific thresholds: wOBA (.025/.050/.075) and FIP (.5/1.0/1.5).

The interval is a broad 80% bootstrap band, not a player-specific probability of success. It measures calibration over the evaluated cohort; it does not explain why a player missed.

Scope and guardrails

report provenance

This report uses the real-data rolling backtest detail from {{ data.scope.source }}. It evaluates players who received observed MLB playing time, not the full population of prospects, and it is not a promotion, health, or playing-time model. Possible causes—role change, park, injury, sample noise—belong in a human follow-up, not in these residuals alone.
