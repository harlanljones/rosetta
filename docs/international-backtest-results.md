# International backtest results (NPB and KBO to MLB)

Generated by `rosetta international-rolling`; the numbers below are copied from its JSON output, do not edit them by hand. Regenerate after any snapshot, factor-model or park-factor change.

## What was run

- Command: `rosetta international-rolling --league <NPB|KBO> --targets 2019-2026 --boot 100` (seed 42, estimator ratio_of_means, minimum 80 PA / 30 IP on both sides).
- For each target season T the factor model is refit on every transferred-player pair (both directions, all leagues) with `to_season < T`. NPB and KBO links are pooled across directions with a direction term and shrink toward the chained AAA product.
- Scored set: exports, meaning players with a real league season in T-1 and a real MLB season in T. The MLB-T line is compared with `translate_line` of the T-1 line.
- Baseline: the T-1 foreign rate carried forward unchanged. Error is prediction minus actual. Coverage is the share of actual outcomes inside the model's 80% band.
- Crosswalk: chadwick. Targets with no exports are recorded as skipped.

## Limitations

- Thin samples. Exports with both-side playing time are few, and KBO movers are rarer still; read the n column before the MAE column. A small pooled n makes both the MAE comparison and the coverage figure noisy, and the gate marks it INSUFFICIENT rather than guessing.
- Snapshot coverage is partial. Real lines in the snapshots (batters plus pitchers, all seasons): KBO 460, NPB 639, MLB 14530. NPB and KBO seasons hold only the players the ingest kept, so exports missing from the snapshots never reach the scored set.
- History is short. The committed snapshots only go back to 2018, so early targets train on very little. The local backfill to 2005 has not run; rerun this after it does.
- Ages are imputed (27.0) for NPB and KBO rows until the age refresh runs, so age adjustment is a no-op for those endpoints and aging differences are absorbed into the factor and the residual spread.
- Park factors are empty until NPB and KBO game logs are scraped: 0 scored pair(s) had a park adjustment. Park effects are therefore inside the error here.
- No fielding or defense data is used here; only rate stats (wOBA, FIP and components) are scored. Grades, WAR and dollars are not validated by this run.
- Selection: only players who reached enough MLB playing time appear. This is not a promotion, injury or playing-time model, and survivors tend to be the better performers.
- Leakage control is by destination season (`to_season < T`). The snapshots are retrospective, not as-of archives, and bootstrap intervals resample rows rather than player clusters (a player can appear in several training seasons).
- Pooled n counts each exported player-season once per stat; a player with several MLB-adjacent seasons can appear in more than one target.
